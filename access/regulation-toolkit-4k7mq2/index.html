The lessons (14 videos)

01

Welcome

A warm introduction to the course, what to expect, and how to get the most out of the toolkit. Start here.

02

The Regulation Tool & Sensory Questionnaire

This is where things start to click. The questionnaire maps your daughter's sensory preferences across eight domains and suggests regulation tools matched to how her nervous system works. Set aside 15 to 20 minutes, somewhere quiet, on a laptop if you can. Complete it together, or on her behalf first and share the results afterwards.

👉 Open the Regulation Tool & Questionnaire
03

Her Nervous System: The ADHD Difference

Why your ADHD girl's nervous system tips out faster, reacts bigger and takes longer to settle. Introduces the Window of Capacity framework that underpins everything in this course.

04

Interoceptive Check-In: Your Body's Early Warning System

Recognise the early signs of dysregulation before it tips into a full meltdown. Introduces S.T.O.P., a simple body check-in she can use anywhere.

05

Shelley's Go-To Regulation Tools

The regulation tools Shelley reaches for most, drawn from clinical practice and her own lived experience as an ADHDer.

06

Widening Her Window of Capacity

Regulation practice doesn't just help in the moment. Over time it widens her window. Here's how that works and what supports it.

07

In the Moment: The After-School Collapse

A practical demo of OT tools and equipment for when she walks through the door and needs to decompress. Pick what resonates and try it this week.

08

In the Moment: Big Emotions and Meltdowns

OT tools and equipment for the big, high-energy moments. Pick one or two that feel right for her and keep them within reach.

09

In the Moment: Starting Homework

Tools to help her settle and get started. Small inputs that make a real difference to task readiness.

10

In the Moment: The Chaotic Morning

Simple things that help shift her into her window of capacity, reduce friction and get her out the door.

11

In the Moment: Regulation in the Classroom

Discreet OT tools she can use at school to shift back into her window without drawing attention to herself.

12

In the Moment: Sleep Readiness

Tools and equipment for the wind-down before bed. Shifting into a lower-energy state and easing the transition to sleep.

13

A Final Note From Shelley

A summary of your key downloads, and a reminder of what this was always about. Not a perfect nervous system. A shared language and a toolkit that fits her.

14

Bonus: A Teen Girl's Perspective. Q&A with Lily

Shelley sits down with Lily, 14, for an honest conversation about what regulation really feels like from the inside. Lily asks what to do when she's too overwhelmed to use any strategy, and what simpler words there are than 'hyperactivated'. Shelley asks what she'd want in her landing zone after school, and whether having a framework for her nervous system actually helps.
