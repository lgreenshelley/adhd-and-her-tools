The lessons (4 videos)

01

Welcome

You're here because something isn't adding up. Maybe you keep snapping at the people you love, shutting down mid-conversation, or hitting a wall by midday. This is where we start to make sense of that. Watch this before the questionnaire. It'll change how you see what's been happening in your body.

02

The Sensory Preferences Questionnaire

The questionnaire maps how your nervous system takes in the world: the noise, the light, the textures, the overwhelm, across all eight sensory domains. Set aside around 20 minutes, somewhere quiet, on a laptop if you can. You'll get a printable PDF of your personalised results at the end.

👉 Open Your Sensory Blueprint
03

Nervous System Regulation in Real Life

Regulation is not about being calm all the time. It's about awareness: noticing what state your nervous system is in and knowing how to gently shift it. What regulation looks like in real life, why your blueprint is one of the fastest tools you have, and how to do an interoceptive check-in.

04

Shelley's Go-To Regulation Tools

Regulation in action. The up-regulation and down-regulation strategies Shelley actually reaches for, grounded in her own sensory blueprint. You don't need a lot of tools. You need the right ones for your nervous system.
