How to use this course

You don't need to do all ten tips at once. In fact, please don't.

  1. Start with one strategy. Choose something that feels doable this week. Maybe a visual schedule, or a short planning session.
  2. Test it for two weeks. Notice what works, what doesn't, and how your child responds.
  3. Tweak and adjust. Every ADHD brain is unique. Modify timing, visuals or sensory supports to match your child's needs and interests.
  4. Add gradually. Once one habit feels familiar, introduce another. Over time these small shifts create meaningful change.
