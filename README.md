## Tools

### My Sensory Blueprint — Women's Edition
**URL:** tools.adhdandher.com.au/sensory-blueprint-women

A self-exploration questionnaire for women and adults with ADHD. Maps sensory preferences across 8 domains and generates personalised up-regulation and down-regulation tools based on individual nervous system patterns.

---

### My Regulation Toolkit — Teen Girls Edition
**URL:** tools.adhdandher.com.au/regulation-toolkit-teens

A sensory preferences questionnaire and regulation toolkit for girls and gender diverse young people aged 11–17 (Grades 6–11) with ADHD. Designed to be completed with a trusted adult or caregiver. Generates personalised regulation tools based on individual sensory preferences and nervous system patterns.
